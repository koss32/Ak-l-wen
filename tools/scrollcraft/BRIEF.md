## Journey and feeling curve
Recognition → choice → trust → clarity → commitment → connection → possibility.
Start: anticipation from strong headline and dimensional gloves. Disciplines: agency through disclosure. Trainers: honesty through labeled anonymous placeholders. Schedule/prices: clarity from confirmed data. Form: control through clear validation and honest demo status. Contacts/about: orientation. VALSET: possibility from original blue and gold illustrated world and specific programs.

Peak: “The boxing gloves gently open up the path to my training.” Opening-to-disciplines transition owns the motion peak; content sections remain naturally sized as required by v2. No authored empty scroll.

## Grammar and score

Bans: forced pinning, changing section order, hidden essential copy, invented content. The eight stock grammars do not preserve this exact approved architecture.
